Meet your guide

Ahmed Hossam

A human potential and performance coach, an entrepreneur, and a co-founder of Retrive.

He works with capable people who sense there is more in them but are busy doing a lot without clear direction. The path he uses, and is still developing and testing, has four steps:

KnowGrowPerformContribute

Understand yourself, develop on purpose, act with intention, and make it useful to others.

He spent more than ten years in design, advertising, marketing, branding and business, and rose to Brand Manager. He also built ventures of his own. Some worked and some didn't. Along the way he trained and competed in martial arts, where practice, pressure, attention and recovery showed him the difference between potential and performance.

To understand the person behind the performance, he studied professional coaching, positive psychology (including positive psychology interventions), NLP and human development. He now applies that work in his coaching, programs and retreats.

He knows what it costs to push without knowing why, and what changes when your ability, identity, direction and work finally fit together. He is multidisciplinary by nature and has stopped treating that as a weakness. He doesn't claim to have life figured out. He is still learning, building and testing these ideas against real life.
